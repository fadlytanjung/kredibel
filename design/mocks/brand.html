A k that has been checked

The mark is two strokes. The white stem is the document: a column of printed numbers. The mint tick is Kredibel reading it a second time and agreeing. Together they make the k of kredibel.

  • The stem is what was printed. Upright, plain, the same width all the way down, like a column in a statement.
  • The tick is the check. It grows out of the middle of the stem, so the check is always attached to a source.
  • The green tile is the ledger. A rounded square that holds both, and the colour of every primary action.

Construction

Drawn on a 64-unit square. Every measure comes from the stem, so the mark scales without drift.

Tile
64 × 64, corner radius 18 (28%). Ledger green.
Stem
9.5 wide, 41 tall, at 14.5 from the left, centred vertically. Fully rounded ends.
Tick
Same 9.5 stroke, round caps and join. Starts at 25.5, 34 so its cap overlaps the stem; lowest point 35, 44.5; ends at 49, 15.5, higher than the stem.
Clear space
One stem width (9.5 units, about 15% of the tile) on every side. Nothing else enters it.
Small sizes
At 32 px and below use favicon.svg: the stem and tick are 11 wide and the radius 16, so they stay open at 16 px.
Lockup
Mark, then a gap of a quarter tile, then the wordmark kredibel in Archivo 720 at 116% width, lowercase, outlined. Cap height lines up with the top of the stem.

Keep the meaning

The tick says a number was checked. Anything that weakens that is off-brand.

  • No red or amber tick. A warning is never shown with the mark.

  • No floating tick. It always grows out of the stem.

  • No rotating, stretching, outlines or shadows.

  • No other tile colours. Partners get a lockup, not a recolour.

Why Kredibel

Books closed by hand, weeks after the fact.

Three things go wrong before anyone notices — and by then they have already been counted.

What goes wrong

  • The same statement is uploaded twice, or a supplier invoice is scanned again, and an expense is counted twice.
  • A PDF can be edited in minutes, and a supplier's account can be a fraudster's.
  • A payment to a new account sits in the books until someone spots it, if at all.

What Kredibel does instead

  • An exact copy is recorded and never read again; a possible copy is held until you decide.
  • Every number is read twice and counted only when both readings agree.
  • Each month reconciles itself, and the few things AI cannot settle wait in one short review queue.

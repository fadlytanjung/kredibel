Screen mocks

High-fidelity, responsive mocks with synthetic data: the demo workspace of docs/18 (Sunrise Coffee Roasters, January to June 2026, scenarios S1 to S4 together). Every link, tab and button leads to another mock, so you can click through them like a prototype, starting at the dashboard. Resize the window: every mock works from 360 px to 1600 px. The rules are in docs/12-design-system.md, the tokens in contracts/design/tokens.json, and the screen specs in docs/11-frontend.md. The HTML is generated by tools/design/mocks/ (make mocks).
